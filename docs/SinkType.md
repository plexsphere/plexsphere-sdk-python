# SinkType

Protocol or backend one sink delivers to. `otlp` and `syslog` are the forwarding protocols a Domain declares for itself; `mimir`, `loki` and `siem` name the platform's own backends and appear on built-in sinks only. 

## Enum

* `OTLP` (value: `'otlp'`)

* `SYSLOG` (value: `'syslog'`)

* `MIMIR` (value: `'mimir'`)

* `LOKI` (value: `'loki'`)

* `SIEM` (value: `'siem'`)

# TelemetrySeverity

Syslog severity keyword, most severe first. A `logs` route states one as its floor and carries every record at least as severe as it. 

## Enum

* `EMERG` (value: `'emerg'`)

* `ALERT` (value: `'alert'`)

* `CRIT` (value: `'crit'`)

* `ERR` (value: `'err'`)

* `WARNING` (value: `'warning'`)

* `NOTICE` (value: `'notice'`)

* `INFO` (value: `'info'`)

* `DEBUG` (value: `'debug'`)
